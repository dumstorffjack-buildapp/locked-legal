Privacy Policy

Locked: The Grind, Tracked · Effective October 31, 2026

The short version: We collect only what Locked needs to work: your account info, your onboarding answers, and your progress (so it survives a reinstall or new phone). Messages you send to the AI Partner go through our server to OpenAI to generate a reply. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. You can delete your account and data from inside the app at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains how Locked ("Locked," "we," "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Locked iOS app (the "App"). Locked is operated by Jack Dumstorff, an individual developer based in Iowa, United States.

Information collected automatically

  • Usage limits: We keep a count of AI Partner requests per account per hour to prevent abuse. These counts expire automatically.
  • Server logs: Like most online services, our server provider (Cloudflare) may process technical information such as IP address and request time to deliver and secure the service.

What we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your payment details. Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple.
  • We do not use advertising, ad tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, or microphone.
  • Notifications and Live Activities are created on your device and don't send data to us.

2. Data stored only on your device

Your AI Partner chat history is stored only on your device. It is not backed up to our servers, so it is deleted if you delete the App. App preferences such as your notification settings are also stored on your device.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide, run, and personalize the App.
  • To back up and restore your progress across reinstalls and devices.
  • To generate AI Partner replies.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service secure.
  • To respond to you if you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information, share it for advertising, or use it to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users or others.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and backed-up progress until you delete your account. When you delete your account, we delete your Firebase account, your onboarding answers, and all backed-up progress data, and the App removes your data from your device. Request counters expire within hours. Copies held by service providers (such as backups or abuse-monitoring logs) are deleted on their own schedules.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and backed-up data.
  • Export your data: Settings → Export Data.
  • Use the App without the AI Partner: You can simply not use it. Nothing is sent to OpenAI unless you send a message or request a plan or suggestions.
  • Notifications: Turn them off any time in the App's Settings or in iOS Settings.
  • Other requests: Depending on where you live (for example California, or the EU/UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. Contact us at the email below and we'll respond within the time required by law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Your backed-up data is stored in Google Firebase and is protected by rules that allow only your signed-in account to read or write it. Your long-term sign-in token is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device. No system is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Where your data is processed

Your information is stored and processed in the United States. If you use the App from outside the U.S., your information will be transferred to and processed in the U.S.

9. Children

Locked is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Not medical advice

Locked is a focus and productivity tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat ADHD or any other condition.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If we make significant changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the App or by other reasonable means.
