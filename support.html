Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Locked → Cancel Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel your subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Apple handles all payments and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I get my streak back?

Sign in with the same account (email or Google) you used before. Your sessions, streaks, missions, and goals restore automatically. To restore your subscription, tap Restore Purchases on the subscription screen.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and data. You'll be asked to confirm your identity first.

The AI Partner isn't replying.

Make sure you're signed in and connected to the internet. The AI Partner has an hourly limit; if you hit it, try again a little later.

I'm not getting notifications.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Locked and make sure they're allowed, then check your notification toggles in Locked's Settings.

In crisis? Locked can't respond to emergencies. If you're thinking about hurting yourself, call or text 988 (U.S.) or your local emergency number right away.
